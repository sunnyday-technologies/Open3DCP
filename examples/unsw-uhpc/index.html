Example entry · ultra-high-performance concrete

UNSW Global UHPC → Open3DCP

How the high end of the strength range — silica-fume UHPC, steel-fibre reinforced, ~130 to ~281 MPa — re-formats into Open3DCP, exercising the SCM, fibre, and multi-mechanical columns the normal-strength benchmarks never reach.

1 · Native structure

Storage medium: a single flat table (.xlsx, 3-row hierarchical header), one row per mix, every constituent in kg/m³. 2,188 mixes from 168 publications in the source; a 19-row, seven-mix excerpt is committed here.
